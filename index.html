What each color does in VRChat

VRChat's theme has six slots, always in this order: Highlight, Icons, Buttons, Background, Fonts, Subtext. You pick one accent; it becomes Icons, and the others are built from it so the UI stays readable.

Icons your accent
Every icon: the menu list, the top bar, the cards on the left and the bottom tabs (dimmed in places). It's the color you see most, so it's the one you choose. It's lightened a little so icons stay clear against the darker colors.
Highlight
Marks what's selected or active: outlines on the selected menu item and theme tile, the check badge, and the background of the bottom tab you hover or press. The tab's icon and label sit on top of it, so it's made a darker shade of your hue. A light Highlight leaves the active tab unreadable, at the cost of slightly dimmer outlines.
Buttons
The panels behind things: the profile cards, menu rows and scrollbar. VRChat darkens it further, so it's a very dark tint of your hue.
Background
The window, top bar and side panel. VRChat darkens it heavily, so you only see a faint tint of it. It stays a neutral dark gray unless you tick the tint option.
Fonts
Normal text: headings, the clock, menu names and card values. Secondary text is the same color, dimmed. It's kept near white so text reads on the dark UI.
Subtext
The small capital labels above values (STATUS, AVATAR, WORLD, FRIENDS) and other captions. They're tiny and dimmed, so Subtext is made a light shade of your hue.

Why not just VRChat's default colors? The built-in Default theme is a separate internal theme. Importing its six colors looks different, and the active tab ends up nearly unreadable.
